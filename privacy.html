Laughlet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Laughlet ("we") makes a joke app for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What we collect

  • No account: Laughlet has no sign-up or login. We don't collect your name, email or contacts.
  • Usage on your device: how many jokes you've seen today, your settings, and recent jokes are stored on your device only.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Third parties

We only share data with the services below, and only as described. Each of them is required, under its own terms and privacy policy, to protect that data at least as well as this policy does. We never sell your data.

  • Google Gemini (AI jokes): only if you allow AI jokes. Each request contains a random topic and recent joke text. No name, email, account or device information is sent. See Google's terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Google AdMob (ads): shows banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. It only uses your advertising identifier if you allow tracking. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store and subscriptions): see https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your choices

  • Turn AI jokes off: Settings > AI-written jokes.
  • Ad tracking: iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EU/UK: Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Notifications: Settings > Daily joke, or iPhone Settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Your data > Erase my saved jokes and settings. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your phone. Subscriptions must be cancelled in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Children

Laughlet is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids category.

Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or the app. We do not keep any of your data on our own servers.
