Subscriptions

Laughlet Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that gives you more jokes per day. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Referral bonus

Inviting friends is optional. Each iCloud account can redeem one friend's code. Bonus jokes are a free promotional extra and have no cash value. We may change or end the referral program.

AI-written jokes

Some jokes are generated by AI and filtered to be family-friendly. AI can still make mistakes. If a joke is offensive, tap Report and it will be removed.
