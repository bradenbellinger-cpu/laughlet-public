Support

Need help, want to report a joke or an ad, or have an idea? Email us at bradenbellinger@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How many jokes do I get?

Free users get 3 jokes a day. Laughlet Pro gets 10. The count resets at midnight on your phone.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Laughlet. Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so we can't cancel or refund them for you. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but don't have it

In the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

The daily joke notification stopped

Open Laughlet once. That queues up the next two weeks of jokes. Also check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Laughlet.

Are the jokes written by AI?

Some are, if you allow it. AI jokes are filtered to be family-friendly, and you can turn them off in Settings. If a joke is offensive, tap Report and it will be removed.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, saved jokes, and settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
